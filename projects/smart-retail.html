Case Study

Retail Management Platform

Enterprise software architecture for retail, warehouse management, POS, inventory, purchasing, reporting and cloud synchronization.

Overview

This project involved the design and implementation of a comprehensive retail management platform supporting multiple stores, centralized inventory, warehouse management, purchasing, point-of-sale terminals and executive reporting.

The system was designed with scalability in mind, allowing independent modules to communicate through clearly defined interfaces while maintaining a unified user experience across all business functions.
