ABOUT

Technology has always been the common thread.

From electronics and digital logic to software architecture, connected systems and product development, my career has developed around understanding how things work — and making them work better.

THE STORY 01

Starting with electronics.

My formal technical background began with electronics and digital logic at the University of Amsterdam.

I studied at the Electronics Department (ETS), completing a Master's Degree in Electronics with a focus on Digital Logic and Logic Design between 1986 and 1990.

That foundation has remained useful throughout my career. Even when my work moved increasingly toward software, I retained an interest in the hardware, devices and physical systems that software ultimately has to interact with.

SOFTWARE 02

From electronics to software systems.

Over time, software became the dominant part of my professional work, particularly backend engineering, architecture and distributed systems.

My experience includes LAMP and LEMP environments, PHP and Laravel, Node.js, JavaScript and Python, together with relational and NoSQL databases.

The focus has increasingly been on the architecture surrounding software: databases, messaging, APIs, infrastructure, integrations and the way individual components become a complete system.

LEADERSHIP 03

Technology is also about people.

Building a system is only part of delivering a product. Teams, priorities, communication and product decisions are equally important.

At Signal Secure I led a cross-functional development team, led Agile ceremonies, helped define product direction and coordinated software development with firmware and hardware teams.

I have also been involved in defining feature specifications, milestones and hiring requirements, onboarding engineers, mentoring junior developers and communicating technical direction to management.

CONNECTED TECHNOLOGY 04

Where software meets the physical world.

This is where my electronics background and software experience naturally come together.

At Signal Secure, I worked on major automotive IoT and smart-cart POS product lines. These systems required backend architecture, telemetry, device control and coordination with firmware and hardware teams.

I enjoy this boundary between disciplines: understanding the physical device, understanding the software, and then designing the system that connects them.
