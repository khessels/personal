Engineering Approach

Every successful product begins with a solid architecture. My development process covers the complete lifecycle, ensuring that software, electronics, mechanics and manufacturing are aligned from day one.

Requirements gathering, feasibility studies, technology selection and risk assessment.

02 — Architecture

Software architecture, electronics architecture, communication protocols, cloud infrastructure and security.

03 — Development

PCB design, firmware, embedded Linux, backend software, web applications and cloud services.

04 — Validation

Functional testing, performance optimization, EMC considerations, manufacturing validation and documentation.

05 — Production

Prototype support, manufacturing, deployment, maintenance and continuous improvement.
